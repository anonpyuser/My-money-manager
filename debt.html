Credit utilization

Utilization is based on the card balances and limits you enter, not your complete credit report. Lower reported balances may help your credit profile, but there is no guaranteed score change or exact score target.

Habits that help

1. Avoid missing due dates.
Use autopay for at least minimums where feasible and keep reminders enabled. A payment history is important for credit health.
2. Reduce revolving balances.
Payments can lower utilization when lenders report new balances. Other report factors may still affect your score.
3. Compare costs, not promised score gains.
Avalanche typically minimizes interest when rates are known; snowball prioritizes smaller balances for momentum. Neither strategy guarantees a particular credit score.
4. Handle overdue and charged-off accounts separately.
Confirm account status and who owns the debt before making a plan. Paying a charged-off debt may not erase prior late-payment reporting. Avoid assuming it will.
5. Keep the numbers accurate.
Statements, fees, daily interest, promotions, new purchases, late charges, and different APR tiers can change the real payoff schedule.

Your data stays local

Debt entries and score notes use the same browser storage as your money manager, so an app backup includes them. They are not uploaded to GitHub. Use Settings → Export backup regularly. The bill planner does NOT automatically add debt minimums from this page: if you also have matching bill entries, do not double-count payments.
